# Voice Room Operations & Triage Guide

## Overview

This guide explains how to join and manage live voice rooms for incident triage using the **AI Incident Commander**.

---

## Quick Start Flow

1. Navigate to `/incidents` to view active incidents.
2. Select an incident or click **"Declare New Incident"**.
3. Inside the incident dashboard, click **"🎙️ Join Voice Room"**.
4. Enter your **Name** and select your **Role** (e.g. `ENGINEER`, `SRE`, `SUPPORT`).
5. Click **"Connect microphone"** and allow browser recording permissions.

---

## Controls

- **Mute / Unmute**: Toggle your local microphone input.
- **Audio Visualizer**: Displays dynamic green waveform bars when you or remote teammates speak.
- **Leave Bridge**: Gracefully disconnects from the audio channel and records departure timestamps.
